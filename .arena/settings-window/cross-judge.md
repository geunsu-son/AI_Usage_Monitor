# Cross-judge: settings window

Judge: Claude (another model family than the parent, composer/auto). I read both DESIGN.md files and the grounding README end to end, and screened them against design-red-flags.md. I also checked one claim against the source with `rg`: `WM_COMMAND` on the widget is produced only by `show_context_menu` (`window.rs:3614`), and `tray_icon::IDM_TOGGLE_WIDGET` is a menu item (`window.rs:4587`) plus the tray left-click (`TrayAction::ToggleWidget`, `window.rs:3947`). No other code posts `WM_COMMAND`.

## Scores

| --- | --- | --- |
| 1. Usage-first fidelity | **5.** The menu, dialog, and tray-click call sites are each shown in a few lines. All three end in `settings::apply(hwnd, cmd)`. Changes apply live, which matches the grounding goal. | **3.** The call sites are shown, but the tray toggle routes through `cycle_focus_from_widget` and `IDM_CYCLE_FOCUS_SENTINEL`, which conflates two flows. ## Base recommendation

**Candidate 1 is the base.** By criterion:

1. **Usage fidelity.** Candidate 1's three call sites match the actual flows I checked in `window.rs`: the menu, the dialog controls, and the tray left-click. Candidate 2 shows a draft-commit flow that the grounding does not ask for, and its focus-cycle and tray-toggle wiring is muddled.
2. **Single apply path.** Both candidates converge on one writer. Only Candidate 1 removes the old path structurally: no `WM_COMMAND` arm, no `IDM_*`. Candidate 2 keeps the id tables and needs a unit test to keep them in sync.
3. **Win32.** Candidate 2 has a disqualifying hazard: a modal dialog owned by an embedded child disables `Shell_TrayWnd`. Candidate 1's residual risks (topmost inheritance, Alt-Tab visibility) are flagged and cheap to settle.
4. **Depth.** Candidate 1's public surface is about six names. Candidate 2's is several dozen, mostly parallel field mirrors, which is the shallow-module and hand-synced-list flags at once.
5. **Laziness.** Candidate 1 adds two child modules and moves nothing out of `window`. Candidate 2 relocates the domain enums and retypes `AppState` as a prerequisite.
6. **Agent maintainability.** Candidate 1 encodes "add a preference" as "add a variant and let exhaustive matches fail the build". Candidate 2 spreads one field across about 8 sites.

## Grafts worth taking from the loser

1. **Pure, testable guard and effect logic.** Candidate 1 already worries that testing `mutate` needs an `AppState` fixture. Take Candidate 2's split: make `Visibility` and an effect-derivation function pure. One option is `fn effects_for(change: &Change, facts: EffectFacts) -> Effects`, with `mutate` doing only the field writes. Then port Candidate 2's test list as-is: last provider and last row refused, Claude unavailable, focus normalization order (Codex, Cursor, Claude), provider change forcing a poll without `has_poll_result`, a poll change retiming only with `has_poll_result`, Alert Off clearing versus Notify, and Reset setting the anchor.
2. **Snap non-preset poll values on load (`PollStep::from_stored`).** This closes Candidate 1's open question: a combo with no selection when `settings.json` holds a non-preset `poll_interval_ms`. Snap inside `normalize_settings` so memory only ever holds a preset. Then `SettingsView.poll_interval` becomes `PollInterval` instead of `Option`, and the same applies to `AlertThreshold`.
3. **Re-check live `claude_code_available` at write time, and keep display identity under one key function.** Candidate 1's `Visibility::of(state)` under the lock already covers the first part; make it an explicit invariant in the `apply` doc. For the second part, take Candidate 2's rule that display identity comes from a single `display_key` helper shared by the read and write paths. That way `DisplayChoice` and `preferred_taskbar_index` can't disagree on the device-or-index fallback.

## Reject with reason

- **Candidate 2's modal `PropertySheetW` with `hwndParent = widget`.** While embedded, this disables Explorer's taskbar for the dialog's lifetime. The nested modal loop also dispatches widget timers re-entrantly. Must not ship.
- **Candidate 2's draft/patch/diff model (`SettingsViewModel`, `SettingsPatch` snapshot, `plan(before, after)`, Apply/Cancel, `PSN_APPLY` dedup).** It creates a second, temporary owner of settings. A full-snapshot commit can overwrite concurrent writes from drag, offset, or system-language follow. It also contradicts the live-apply goal.
- **Candidate 2's `ToggleX` variants alongside `SetX`, and `IDM_CYCLE_FOCUS_SENTINEL`.** Two ways to express one change, plus a fake menu id used as an internal routing token.
- **Candidate 2's relocation of `LanguageId`, `Appearance`, and the appearance enums into `settings_vm`, and the `AppState` retyping, as part of this change.** This is scope creep. It is only defensible as its own later refactor.
- **Candidate 2's "strip `WS_EX_LAYERED`/`WS_EX_NOACTIVATE` in `PSCB_INITIALIZED`".** It defends against styles that are never set, which hides intent.
- **From Candidate 1: shipping before the owner and topmost question is answered.** Decide owner = widget versus `None` plus `WS_EX_APPWINDOW` with a quick spike before the dialog commit. Also don't fan the new `Strings` fields out to all 11 locales in the MVP. Ship English plus the existing en/zh labels with an English fallback, and add the other locales as a follow-up.
- **From Candidate 1: leaving `set_window_title` under the lock "as today".** It's acceptable for the first commit, but log it as a known follow-up so it doesn't become precedent for Win32 calls inside `mutate`.
